Graphoooについて

Graphoooは、項目と数を入れるだけでグラフが作れるiPhoneアプリです。棒グラフ、折れ線グラフ、円グラフを作り、画像にして共有できます。

About Graphooo

Graphooo is an iPhone app that lets you create charts simply by entering labels and numbers. You can create bar charts, line charts, and pie charts, and share them as images.

For questions or bug reports, please email the address below. Including your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue helps us look into it.
